Major Scale Chord Player

A calm, legato piano practice companion. Each number plays its corresponding single note in the current major scale; a tonic major triad frames every sequence.

Practice range

Start and end are the roots of the keys, not the highest note played. Practical piano range: A0–C8.

Saved sequences

Saving or editing never needs an account. Your library is stored using local browser storage.

Sequence editor

Each entered number plays one note from the current major scale (e.g. 2 plays re). Only the opening and closing events are tonic major triads. Repeated numbers are played as repeated legato notes.
